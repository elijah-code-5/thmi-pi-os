T-HMI Hub

  1. Plug the T-HMI into this computer with USB (use Chrome or Edge).
  2. Click the button, pick the port, and wait for it to finish.
  3. On the T-HMI, join the THMI-Setup Wi-Fi from your phone and pick your home Wi-Fi.

If no port appears, hold BOOT while plugging in the cable, then try again.
